1. Overview

Tariq Niaz (“Developer”, “we”, “our”, or “us”) built the Jelly Arrow Escape mobile puzzle game for Android. This Privacy Policy explains what data is collected or processed when you play Jelly Arrow Escape, how third-party advertising, analytics, and Google Play billing services operate, and how you can manage your privacy and consent choices at any time.

For players in the European Economic Area (EEA), the United Kingdom, and Switzerland, Jelly Arrow Escape uses Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) to present a GDPR consent notice before initializing advertising SDKs. In addition, the app provides an explicit Welcome & Privacy consent prompt on first launch and ongoing controls inside Settings → Privacy.

2. Personal Information We Do NOT Collect Directly

Jelly Arrow Escape does not require account creation or user sign-in, and Tariq Niaz does not directly collect, sell, rent, or store sensitive personally identifiable information (PII) such as:

  • Your real name, mailing address, or telephone number
  • Your email address (unless you voluntarily email minidroidhelp@gmail.com for player support or a data deletion request)
  • Precise GPS location coordinates
  • Contacts, device photos, camera video, microphone audio, SMS messages, or external files
  • Payment card or banking details (all in-app purchases are handled directly by Google Play)

3. Advertising, In-App Purchases & Automated Services

A. Third-Party Advertising & Mediation (Unity LevelPlay & Google AdMob)

To keep Jelly Arrow Escape free to play, the app displays advertisements (including Banner, Interstitial, optional Rewarded Video, and optional App Open ads) mediated through Unity LevelPlay and served by authorized third-party advertising partners:

  • Mediation & Ad Partners: Unity LevelPlay / ironSource Ads, Unity Ads, Google AdMob / Google Ad Manager, InMobi, Liftoff Monetize (Vungle), Meta Audience Network, Yandex Ads, and optional BIGO Ads.
  • Data Processed by Ad Partners: Depending on your regional privacy consent (GDPR / UMP / CCPA) and Android device ad settings, these partners may collect and process your Android Advertising ID (AD_ID), IP-derived approximate location (country/region/city), device model, OS version, app version, ad impressions, clicks, crash/diagnostic signals, and fraud-prevention signals to deliver, cap, measure, and personalize (or serve non-personalized) advertisements.
  • Consent & Opt-Out: EEA/UK/Switzerland players can grant, customize, or withdraw advertising consent through Google UMP. All Android users can also reset or delete their Advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads in Android Settings → Google → Ads.

B. In-App Purchases & Subscriptions (Google Play Billing)

Jelly Arrow Escape offers optional in-app purchases (such as coin packs, booster bundles, the permanent All-Levels Pass, and optional Weekly or Monthly Ad-Free Pass subscriptions that remove Banner, Interstitial, and App Open ads). All transactions are processed securely through Google Play Billing (com.android.vending.BILLING). We receive only non-sensitive purchase confirmation tokens/entitlements to unlock your items and never access your credit card or payment credentials. Subscriptions renew automatically unless canceled at least 24 hours before the end of the billing period in Google Play Store → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions.

C. Google Firebase Analytics, Remote Config, Messaging & Player Feedback

Subject to your consent choices, Jelly Arrow Escape uses Google Firebase services (firebase-analytics, firebase-installations, firebase-messaging, and Firebase Remote Config) with Google Consent Mode v2 (AnalyticsStorage, AdStorage, AdUserData, AdPersonalization) to measure level difficulty balance, technical stability, and app configuration:

  • Anonymous Identifiers: A randomly generated local installation identifier (AE_Analytics_UserId) and a Firebase App-Instance / Installation ID.
  • Gameplay & Diagnostics Events: Level starts, completions, booster usage, ad impression/load health, purchase events, and app stability metrics.
  • Optional Player Feedback: If you voluntarily submit written feedback inside the in-app Feedback modal, the message, star rating, and basic non-personal app/device metadata (app version, level number, OS version) are transmitted securely to our Firebase database (player_feedback) solely to fix bugs and improve gameplay.
  • Optional Notifications: When enabled by the player, Unity Mobile Notifications and Google Firebase Cloud Messaging deliver optional puzzle reminders honoring local Quiet Hours (10:00 PM – 9:30 AM). You can disable notifications anytime in Settings → Privacy → Notifications: OFF or Android system settings.

D. Local Game Saves & Android Permissions

Unlocked levels, best times, stars, virtual coin balances, booster counts, and audio/vibration preferences are saved locally on your device using Unity PlayerPrefs. Permissions declared in the Android manifest serve strictly the functions above:

  • INTERNET, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE & ACCESS_WIFI_STATE: Advertising mediation, Google Play Billing, consented Firebase analytics/remote configuration, and optional notifications (core puzzle levels remain playable offline).
  • com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID: Android Advertising ID access for ad serving, frequency capping, attribution, and fraud prevention subject to your consent and OS settings.
  • com.android.vending.BILLING: Optional in-app purchases and subscriptions via Google Play Billing.
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS & RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED: Optional Android 13+ notification reminders when enabled by the user.
  • VIBRATE: Optional tactile haptic feedback (controllable in Settings → Vibration).

5. Data Retention, Opt-Out & Data Deletion Instructions

How to Opt Out or Delete Your Data Anytime:
  1. Instant In-App Controls (Settings → Privacy):
    • Tap Analytics: ON / OFF to immediately disable Firebase Analytics collection and update Consent Mode v2 signals.
    • Tap Notifications: ON / OFF to cancel scheduled local notifications and stop FCM topic/token registration.
    • Tap Delete analytics ID to immediately wipe the local telemetry summary file (arrow_escape_analytics.json) and analytics counters, and rotate your local anonymous ID.
  2. Advertising ID & Ad Personalization Controls: Open your device’s Android Settings → Google → Ads to reset or delete your Advertising ID at any time.
  3. Uninstall & Automatic Expiration: Uninstalling Jelly Arrow Escape deletes all locally stored progress and telemetry files from your device. Firebase Installation IDs automatically expire on Google servers after 270 days of inactivity.
  4. Direct Email Deletion Request: Email Tariq Niaz at minidroidhelp@gmail.com with the subject line Data Deletion Request - Jelly Arrow Escape. We process all verified deletion requests within 7 business days.
