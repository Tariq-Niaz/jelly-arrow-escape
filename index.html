500 progressive 2D levels featuring 50 iconic silhouette formations—including Hearts, Royal Crowns, Butterflies, Snowflakes, and Spiral Mazes—with pinch-to-zoom controls.

500 rotatable 3D cube and stacked cube-on-cube tower levels. Swipe 360° around every face to spot unblocked routes and clear each sculpture.

3. 3D Arrow Board

500 isometric 3D stepped ziggurat puzzles packed with glossy jelly arrows. Orbit and clear multi-directional paths in the right order.
