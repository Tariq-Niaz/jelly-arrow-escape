1. Overview

Tariq Niaz (“Developer”, “we”, “our”, or “us”) built the Jelly Arrow Escape mobile puzzle game for Android. This Privacy Policy informs players regarding our policies with respect to data access, collection, use, disclosure, retention, and user privacy controls when using Jelly Arrow Escape.

On your first launch, Jelly Arrow Escape presents an explicit Welcome & Privacy consent prompt (ACCEPT & PLAY or ESSENTIAL ONLY (OPT OUT)). You can inspect or change your analytics and notification preferences at any time inside the app via Settings → Privacy.

2. Personal Information We Do NOT Collect

Jelly Arrow Escape does not require account creation or user sign-in, and Tariq Niaz does not directly collect, sell, rent, or store personally identifiable information (PII) such as:

  • Your real name, mailing address, or telephone number
  • Your email address (unless you voluntarily send an email to minidroidhelp@gmail.com for player support or a data deletion request)
  • Precise or coarse GPS location coordinates
  • Contacts, device photos, camera video, microphone audio, SMS messages, or external files

3. Advertising, Purchases & Automated Telemetry

A. Ad-Free & No Real-Money Purchases (Version 1.0)

  • No Third-Party Ads: Release 1.0 of Jelly Arrow Escape does not integrate third-party advertising networks (such as AdMob or Unity Ads) and does not show third-party advertisements.
  • No Real-Money In-App Billing: The app does not process real-money purchases (com.android.vending.BILLING is not used). Boosters and items in the in-game Jelly Shop use virtual coins earned through gameplay.

If optional rewarded advertisements or in-app purchases are added in a future release, this Privacy Policy, consent dialogs, and Google Play Data Safety disclosures will be updated prior to release.

B. Google Firebase Analytics & Google Consent Mode v2

Subject to your consent choice, Jelly Arrow Escape uses Google Firebase Analytics (firebase-analytics / firebase-installations) with Google Consent Mode v2 (AnalyticsStorage, AdStorage, AdUserData, AdPersonalization) solely to evaluate level difficulty balance, technical stability, and game progression:

  • Anonymous Device or Other IDs: A randomly generated local installation identifier (AE_Analytics_UserId) and a Firebase App-Instance / Installation ID. Additionally, Firebase Analytics merges standard Google Play Services measurement/attribution APIs (AD_ID, ACCESS_ADSERVICES_ATTRIBUTION, ACCESS_ADSERVICES_AD_ID, BIND_GET_INSTALL_REFERRER_SERVICE) used strictly for aggregate analytics and install attribution — never for serving behavioral ads.
  • App Activity & Interactions: Selected game mode (classic_2d, cube_3d, board_3d), level events (level_start, level_mistake, level_complete, level_fail, player_drop_off), booster actions (powerup_used), virtual currency changes (currency_earned, currency_spent), zoom/pan usage (camera_navigation_used), session counts, and notification interaction events.
  • App Info & Device Diagnostics: Device model, operating system version, platform, and application version used to diagnose device compatibility.
  • Approximate Location (Country / City Level Only): Derived automatically by Google Firebase from anonymized/masked IP addresses during transmission; GPS location is never accessed.

C. Optional Local & Push Notifications

When enabled by the player, Jelly Arrow Escape uses Unity Mobile Notifications and Google Firebase Cloud Messaging (firebase-messaging) to schedule local puzzle reminders and receive optional puzzle updates across general topics (all_players, puzzle_challenges). Automated notifications honor device local Quiet Hours (10:00 PM – 9:30 AM). You can disable notifications at any time via Settings → Privacy → Notifications: OFF or Android system settings.

D. Local Game Saves & Android Permissions

Unlocked levels, best times, stars, virtual coin balances, booster counts, and audio/vibration preferences are saved locally on your device using Unity PlayerPrefs. Permissions declared in the Android manifest serve strictly the functions above:

  • INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Consented anonymous Firebase telemetry and optional FCM delivery (core puzzle gameplay works 100% offline).
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS, RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED, WAKE_LOCK: Optional Android 13+ notification reminders when enabled by the user.
  • VIBRATE: Optional tactile haptic feedback (controllable in Settings → Vibration).

4. Third-Party Service Providers & Security

When telemetry or push messaging is enabled, data is processed through Google infrastructure subject to Google’s own privacy commitments:

  • Google Play Services: https://www.google.com/policies/privacy/
  • Google Firebase Privacy & Security: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy

All network communications between Jelly Arrow Escape and Google Firebase / Google Play Services are encrypted in transit using HTTPS / TLS. We never sell personal user data to third parties.

5. Data Retention, Opt-Out & Data Deletion Instructions

How to Opt Out or Delete Your Data Anytime:
  1. Instant In-App Controls (Settings → Privacy):
    • Tap Analytics: ON / OFF to immediately disable Firebase Analytics collection and update Consent Mode v2 signals.
    • Tap Notifications: ON / OFF to cancel scheduled local notifications and stop FCM topic/token registration.
    • Tap Delete analytics ID to immediately wipe the local telemetry summary file (arrow_escape_analytics.json) and analytics counters, and rotate your local anonymous ID.
  2. Uninstall & Automatic Expiration: Uninstalling Jelly Arrow Escape deletes all locally stored progress and telemetry files from your device. Firebase Installation IDs automatically expire on Google servers after 270 days of inactivity.
  3. Direct Email Deletion Request: Email Tariq Niaz at minidroidhelp@gmail.com with the subject line Data Deletion Request - Jelly Arrow Escape. We process all verified deletion requests within 7 business days.

6. Children’s Privacy

Jelly Arrow Escape is designed for a general puzzle audience (ages 13+ / general casual players) and does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13 (or the applicable minimum age in your jurisdiction). If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided personal contact details to us via support email, please contact minidroidhelp@gmail.com and we will delete the information immediately.
